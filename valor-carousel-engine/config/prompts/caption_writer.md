Caption voice guidance (used inline by the slide planner):

- First-person, conversational, confident but not hypey.
- Open with a line that echoes slide 1's hook without repeating it word-for-word.
- Middle: 2–3 short sentences that expand the story, referencing what slides
  2–5 show.
- Product mention: ONCE, naturally, positioned around 70% through the caption.
  Format: "I packed all of this into {PRODUCT_NAME} if you want the shortcut."
- Close with the brief's CTA, one short line.
- Hashtags on their own line at the very end, space-separated.
- Total length 150–400 characters INCLUDING hashtags.
- No emojis in the middle of sentences. At most one emoji at the end of the
  CTA line.
